## What Text I Analyzed 
I analyzed five different Burno Mars songs all from different albums. From Doo-Wops and Hooligans, I analyzed Talking to the Moon, from Unorthodox Jukebox I analyzed When I Was Your Man, from 24k Magic I analyzed Too Good to Say Goodbye, from An Evening with Silk Sonic I analyzed Put On a Smile, and from the Romantic I analyzed Risk It All. 

## What Questions I Asked
The questions I asked were which song has the most unique vocabulary, what is the emotional tone of each song, and how do the songs compare across albums? 

## What I Found
I found that Put on the Smile has the most Unique words but it also has the most words. When I Was Your Man has the highest vocabulary ratio of 0.43. Too Good to Say Goodbye has the highest vibe score. The most sad song is Put on a Smile and the most love song is Too Good To Say Goodbye. 

## What Suprised Me
I am suprised that When I was Your Man was scored as a happy song. Based on the lyrics, it seems as if it should be categorized as sad.
