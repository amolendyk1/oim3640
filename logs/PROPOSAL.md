# milestone 0 - My project proposal 
What am I building: 
- I am building an app that converts currency.
  
Why I chose this: 
- I chose this app because when I have traveled to other countries, I found it annoying/tedious to look on Google to see how much things cost while I am in other countries. In addition, it will make it easier for online shopping in other countries and to send money if you are in different countries from your family or friends. 
  
- Core features: 
  - conversion rates (including automatic updates when they change)
  - can be used for all currencies
  - does not require data (especially in other countries when wifi and connection are not stable)
  - change in exchange rates over time (to help you see if they increase and decrease)

What I don't Know yet:
- I do not know how to make it so the app automatically updates the exchange rates when they change. I also do not know how to make it so the app works offline.
